Half a century at the bench.

Welcome to Gosnells Smash Repairs. We are an independent, insurance approved and preferred auto body workshop at 21 Carole Rd, Maddington — trusted by motorists across Perth's south-east since 1973.

Our team's combined experience spans panel beating, welding and re-spraying on everything from minor dents to major structural repair. The standard never changes: do the work properly, then stand behind it.
